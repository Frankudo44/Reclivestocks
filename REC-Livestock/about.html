Our Story

Committed to Nigerian Agriculture

REC Livestock & Agro Farms Enterprises is a registered partnership business dedicated to producing and supplying quality poultry, livestock and farm products across Nigeria.

We are committed to helping farmers, families and businesses get reliable farm products and professional service. From healthy day-old chicks and fresh eggs to goats, ram and catfish, we take pride in raising and supplying products that families and businesses can trust.
