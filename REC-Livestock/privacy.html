Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how REC Livestock & Agro Farms Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information you provide when using this website.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit: name, phone number, email address, state, LGA, delivery address and order notes.
  • Order details, including products ordered, quantities and order references.
  • Account details (if you create an account): email address and encrypted authentication credentials (handled by our secure database provider).
  • Technical information such as device type and pages visited, where provided by the hosting platform.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfil your orders and arrange delivery.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery or enquiry.
  • To improve our products, services and website experience.

How we protect your information

We store data in a secure cloud database (Supabase) with row-level security policies restricting access. We never store your password in plain text. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Sharing

We only share information with delivery personnel or service providers strictly as needed to fulfil your order.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through the Contact page.

Cookies & local storage

This website may use browser storage (cookies/local storage) to keep your cart and preferences on your device. You can clear these at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reclivestockagrofarms@gmail.com or WhatsApp us from the Contact page.
